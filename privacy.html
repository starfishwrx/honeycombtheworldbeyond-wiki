Information We Collect

This site is a static website. We do not collect personally identifiable information directly. However, we use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Analytics (if enabled): Collects anonymized page view data. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

This site itself does not set first-party cookies. Third-party services (Google AdSense, Analytics) may set cookies according to their own policies.
