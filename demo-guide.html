Steam Demo Walkthrough

The free 1-hour Steam demo was available August 31, 2026. This guide covers what to do in the demo, how to maximize what you scan, and what carries over to the full game.

About the Demo

Frozen Way released a free 1-hour playable demo for Honeycomb: The World Beyond on Steam on August 31, 2026 — exactly one week before the full game launch (September 8, 2026). The demo covers the opening section of the game and is the basis for much of this wiki's confirmed information.

ℹ️ Demo Status

The demo is/was available on the Steam store page as a separate download. Availability post-launch may vary. Even if the demo is no longer active, this guide documents what the demo contained for wiki completeness.

Demo Scope: What's Included

  • The opening landing sequence on Sota7 and Hennessy's initial briefing from EON Corp
  • The full Amber Plains starting zone — all species and resources accessible
  • The River Crossing biome (reachable within 10–15 minutes)
  • The Open Meadow / Watering Hole area with herd fauna interactions
  • Access to the Bio-Scanner, secateurs, and chisel from the start
  • Enough materials to build: Starter Shelter + Storage Unit + Greenhouse (Lab materials may require extra grinding)
  • Partial Lab access — you can build the Lab but the crossbreeding menu is limited to 1–2 demo-exclusive pairings

What's NOT in the Demo

  • Purple Canopy Forest biome (gated to full game)
  • Crystal Caverns (full game only)
  • Underwater / Abyssal biome (full game only)
  • Multi-generation crossbreeding chains
  • EON Corp story missions beyond the opening objectives

Optimal Demo Walkthrough (60 Minutes)

Minutes 0–5: Landing and Orientation

The demo opens with Hennessy landing via drop-pod in the Amber Plains. Skip cutscenes on a second playthrough. Immediately open your map to orient yourself. The main starting area is a broad sandy plain with orange stalk plants. Locate the distant stone arch to your northeast — that's your river crossing waypoint.

Minutes 5–12: Water First

Head to the river crossing. Drink immediately to refill Thirst. Scan the river lily-pads and the red dragonflies flying overhead. Collect 3–4 plant specimens from the river bank using secateurs. Do NOT enter the water yet.

Minutes 12–22: Herd Interaction

Follow the river south to the Open Meadow. The herd fauna congregate here. You can scan each individual in the herd separately — each unique specimen counts. The teal-fin lizard is found at the waterhole edge. This section alone can fill 40% of your Research Progress meter if you're thorough.

The demo's most memorable moment: crouch near a herd beast and wait. Within 30 seconds, it will walk over and nuzzle your hand (screenshot_5 moment). This triggers a Field Journal entry.

Minutes 22–35: Resource Gathering

Return to the Amber Plains and systematically harvest materials. Target: 12× plant fiber, 8× stone chip (from rock cluster near the arch), 6× edible fruit (from low scrub bushes). Avoid the orange crab-fauna near the eastern rock cluster — scan them from distance only. Collect orange stalk plants last as they're more exposed terrain.

Minutes 35–48: Build Your Base

Select a flat area with good sightlines (recommended: the elevated clearing between the staircase rocks and the purple-leaf tree cluster you can see in screenshot_1). Enter planning mode and place: Shelter → Storage → Greenhouse → (attempt Lab if you have materials). Confirm placement and let the build complete.

Minutes 48–58: First Crossbreeding Attempt

If you built the Lab: enter it, open the crossbreeding menu, and run the one unlocked Grafting experiment (the demo pre-selects two compatible species). Watch the scanning hologram animation — this is the only confirmed demo crossbreeding scene. The resulting hybrid appears in your Field Journal with a unique entry.

Minutes 58–60: Explore Amber Plains Fully

Use remaining time to scan any unscanned specimens — particularly the purple-burst spike plant near the sandy section. The demo does not have a hard timer cutoff (the "1-hour demo" is approximate) — you can continue until you close the game.

📝 Demo Progress

Demo save data does NOT carry over to the full game. Your Field Journal entries, base, and crossbreeding history reset. However, playing the demo is excellent preparation — familiarity with controls and scanning routes makes your full-game first hour significantly more efficient.

Demo Tips: Species to Scan for Maximum Research Progress

These 8 targets, all reachable in the demo area, give the highest combined Research Progress in the shortest time:

  1. Herd Beast (scan all 5+ individuals at the watering hole)
  2. Teal-Fin Lizard (watering hole edge)
  3. Red Dragonfly (river crossing overhead)
  4. River Lily-Pad (river bank)
  5. Low-Scrub Fruit Bush (Amber Plains)
  6. Orange Stalk Plant (Amber Plains open area)
  7. Masked Ground Creature (forest edge near base area)
  8. Purple-Burst Spike Plant (sandy sub-section of Amber Plains)

Completing all 8 should max out the demo's Research Progress meter — which unlocks the final demo Field Journal entry and a pre-launch exclusive EON Corp message about the full game.
